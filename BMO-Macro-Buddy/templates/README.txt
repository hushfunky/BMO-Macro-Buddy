Put template images here for 1280x720 Roblox windows. Resolution-specific captures for 1920x1080 belong in
1920x1080/; those images are preferred automatically at that resolution, with missing images falling back here.

World / Act templates:
- <world>_world.png: world card (candy, ice, fire, lemon, crystal, factory, nightosphere)
- act_<world>_<n>.png: numbered act's stage-name text
- act_<n>_check.png: selected numbered act indicator
- act_endless.png / act_endless_check.png: Endless card / selected indicator
- act_play_again.png: replay button after an act
- lobby_2.png: Lobby button when the word LOBBY isn't visible

Run "python macro.py --check" or press CHECK SETUP in BMO to verify templates and resolution.
